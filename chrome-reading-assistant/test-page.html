What is Reading Progress Saver?

Reading Progress Saver is a Chrome extension that automatically saves your reading progress across websites and restores it when you return. It's perfect for long articles, documentation, or any web content where you might lose your place.

How it Works

The extension tracks your scroll position as you read and saves it to Chrome's local storage. When you return to the same page, it automatically scrolls to where you left off, making it easy to continue reading from exactly where you stopped.

Key Features

  • Automatic Tracking: Saves progress as you scroll
  • Smart Restoration: Returns you to your exact reading position
  • Visual Feedback: Shows a notification when progress is restored
  • Progress Management: View and manage all saved progress
  • Privacy-First: All data stored locally in your browser
